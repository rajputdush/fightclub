Before you send it

What you are agreeing to

  1. The purse is real

    Both of you put the full amount in the referee's hands before the bell.

  2. Winner takes it all

    No cut for the house, no split for a draw. A draw means the purse goes back to both fighters.

  3. Either of you can stop

    A tap ends the fight and the other fighter wins. No arguing at the door.

  4. Same weight class

    If the scale disagrees with your slip, the referee changes your opponent.

Fightin is a sparring club concept. Padded, supervised and consensual. 18 and over. Purses are agreed between the fighters and the referee. This site does not hold or send money.
