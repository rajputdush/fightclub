Boxing sparring gear checklist

The right equipment should fit, be in good condition, and meet your gym’s rules. Check requirements with your coach before buying anything.

Bring the essentials

  • Hand wraps: Learn to wrap your hands properly and use wraps that fit comfortably under your gloves.
  • Boxing gloves: Ask your coach which size and type are suitable for the session. Check the padding, seams, and fastening before training.
  • Mouthguard: Use a clean guard that fits securely. Do not share mouthguards.
  • Protective equipment: Follow your coach’s and organisation’s requirements for head, groin, or chest protection.
  • Water and a towel: Bring your own, and follow your gym’s hygiene guidance for shared equipment.

Check fit and condition

Equipment that slips, causes pain, or has damaged padding needs attention before a round begins. If you are unsure whether your gear is suitable, ask your coach to check it. Equipment standards can vary by activity and governing body, so a generic online checklist cannot replace your club’s rules.

Before you buy

Ask your gym what it requires, whether it has equipment you can try, and how glove size is chosen for your session. Fit and suitability depend on the boxer and the type of training. A coach can help you avoid buying equipment that does not meet the gym’s rules.

For a beginner-focused overview of getting started and equipment, see England Boxing’s getting-started guide. Requirements differ by organisation and location.

Fightin Club India is presented as a sparring club concept; this article is general information and does not list requirements for a live class.
