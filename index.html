Start to finish

How a fight works

  1. Post a purse

    Pick a weight class and an amount. Say when and where you can fight.

  2. Get a taker

    Another fighter accepts. The challenge is locked and nobody else can take it.

  3. Fight

    Three rounds, gloves on, a doctor close by. Both fighters hand the purse to the referee before the bell.

  4. Collect

    The referee calls the winner and the winner takes the whole purse. The house keeps nothing.

Pinned to the door

House rules

Break one and you are off the board for a month. Break two and the group chat finds out.

  1. Gloves stay on

    Bare knuckles belong in movies. Ours go home intact.

  2. Tap means done

    A tap, a word or a wave ends the round. Nobody asks why.

  3. Go easy on heads

    You have a meeting on Monday and so does your opponent.

  4. Purse before bell

    If the money is not with the referee, the fight does not start.

  5. Stop on the bell

    First time, every time. The bell is louder than your pride.

  6. Shake hands after

    Win or lose, you thank the person who just let you hit them.
