About these guides

Fightin Club India is currently presented as a sparring club concept. The sample fight cards, fighters, and schedules elsewhere on this site are examples, not live events. These articles are general educational information; follow qualified local coaching and applicable rules.

Fightin is a sparring club concept. Padded, supervised and consensual. 18 and over. Purses are agreed between the fighters and the referee. This site does not hold or send money.
