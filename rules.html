House rules

Short enough to read at the door. Strict enough that everyone goes home on their own feet.

Pinned to the door

The six

  1. Gloves stay on

    Bare knuckles belong in movies. Ours go home intact.

  2. Tap means done

    A tap, a word or a wave ends the round. Nobody asks why.

  3. Go easy on heads

    You have a meeting on Monday and so does your opponent.

  4. Purse before bell

    If the money is not with the referee, the fight does not start.

  5. Stop on the bell

    First time, every time. The bell is louder than your pride.

  6. Shake hands after

    Win or lose, you thank the person who just let you hit them.

Instant disqualification

Fouls

Any of these ends the fight. The other fighter wins and takes the purse.

  • Hitting after the bellOr after a tap
  • Strikes to the back of the headOr the spine
  • Strikes below the beltEven by accident, twice
  • Biting, gouging, grabbing the ropesSelf-explanatory
  • Fighting under the influenceThe referee decides

About the money

Purse rules

  1. Agree first

    The amount is fixed when the challenge is accepted. It cannot change at the door.

  2. Hand it over

    Both fighters give the full purse to the referee at 8:30 PM, before weigh-in.

  3. Winner collects

    The referee pays the winner after the last bout. The house keeps nothing.

  4. Draws and no-shows

    A draw returns each purse. If someone does not show, the other fighter gets their money back.

Everyone goes home
