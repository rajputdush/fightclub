At the door

Good to know

How does the money work?

One fighter posts a purse and another accepts. Both hand the full amount to the referee before the bell, and the winner takes all of it. The house keeps nothing.

Does this website handle payments?

No. The site shows challenges and results. Money is handed over in person to the referee, and it is paid out the same way.

Do I have to be good to fight?

No. You are matched by weight and by experience, and the referee will not put a first-timer against someone with ten wins.

Who can come?

Anyone aged 18 or over with a membership card. Walk-ins are turned away at the door, so make your card first.

What if I get hurt?

A doctor is ringside all night. A tap ends any fight, and the referee or doctor can stop it at any time.

What do I need to bring?

A mouth guard, hand wraps, gloves and water. Headgear is provided if you forget yours.

Can I just watch?

Yes. Members can bring one guest to the edge of the ring. Guests do not fight and do not hold the purse.

What happens in a draw?

Each fighter gets their own purse back, and the two of you can schedule a rematch.
